# Inventory MVP — implementation tasks

## Objective
Build the first reviewable foundation for a single-company, multi-branch inventory app on Android, iOS, and internal web. Maintain branch-scoped stock, trusted online mutations, immutable movement history, and admin-only purchase costs.

## Accepted scope and constraints
- Flutter project: `lda_inventory`; organization: `com.ldasoftware`.
- Firebase Auth, Firestore, Functions, Storage, Hosting, Security Rules, and emulators are the proposed stack. User chose local emulators first, then a new development Firebase project in Dallas (`us-south1`) for Firestore, Storage and future gen2 Functions; a distinct production project/location is deferred until MVP validation. Development data may reside in the United States. Development starts on no-cost Spark, with no Cloud Billing account linked; Cloud Functions deploys and Cloud Storage are unavailable until a separate user-approved Blaze upgrade. User selected their personal Google account as development project owner; the isolated Firebase CLI now has one authenticated personal account; development Firebase project `lda-inventory-dev-mx` is ACTIVE on Spark (user-confirmed in console) with Firestore `(default)` in `us-south1`; Firestore rules deployed in development; Android/web FlutterFire configuration works locally, and the iOS client app is registered but its local integration is blocked on missing Xcode/Ruby tooling. Never touch production without separate authorization.
- Initial product sale price is global and administrator-editable with an audit trail; sales preserve their actual price. Branch overrides are future work.
- Current acquisition-cost reference is the latest entry cost; each entry retains its actual historical cost. Weighted average, margins, and profit reports are future work.
- Sellers have branch-limited access and cannot read acquisition costs. Inventory writes are online, transactional, idempotent, and cannot make available stock negative.
- MVP includes catalog and QR identity, stock intake, sales and internal receipts, partial returns, exchanges, warranty states, immediate branch transfers, and destination in-app notices. Excludes offline writes, suppliers, credit, fiscal integration, public commerce, refund reconciliation, and external invoice references.
