## Specs
- S1. Scan to search. User (parent doc S6, verbatim): "Meter alguna especie de buscador dentro del inventario" and "sería conveniente tambien por QR poder generar la busqueda además de por nombre". The QR payload of a product is its internal code (`lib/catalog/product_qr.dart`, `ProductQr.payloadFor`), and T6 already searches by that code, so a scanned payload is typed into the search field and the existing matcher filters the list. No new lookup path.
- S2. Platform and behaviour decisions taken with the user on 2026-10-20 (choices quoted verbatim): "1. Solo Android e iOS (según S6)" — the camera entry point exists only on Android and iOS, web keeps the typed search only; "2. Bundled (inkluido en la APK)" — Android uses the bundled ML Kit barcode scanner (works offline and on devices without Google Play Services, at the cost of APK size), so no `useUnbundled` flag; "3. Un escaneo y cierra" — one successful scan fills the search field and closes the camera.
- S3. Failure and permission copy in Spanish, and no silent behaviour: denying the camera, a device without camera, or a barcode that is not a QR must show what happened and how to continue. T7 does not change what a query matches (T6/S1).
